Working at El Peñón requires the authorization of the Club's Technical Committee.

The following are lists of pilots who:
- have been formally authorized by the Club's Technical Committee,
- **chose to appear publicly on these lists**, and
- in the case of Instructors, Assistants and Tandem Pilots, are current Club Members holding a valid membership.

:::caution
These lists only include pilots who chose to appear publicly. A pilot who is not listed may still be authorized.
:::
