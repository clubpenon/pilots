For the injured pilot and for anyone who witnesses an emergency.

## Protocol

- **Report the emergency**
   - Radio: 145.050 MHz
   - Phone: [+52 722 933 9189](tel:+527229339189) (volunteer group)
   - Say:
     - **Who:** your name and phone
     - **Where:** pilot's location
     - **How bad:** unconscious? life-threatening? injuries
     - **Contact:** how to reach the pilot (phone, radio)
   - **Unconscious or life-threatening:** also call [911](tel:911) and activate your inReach/SPOT SOS.
- **If you are injured:** stay reachable by phone and radio. Do not move if you suspect a back, neck or hip injury.
- **If you are a witness:** go to the pilot with a first aid kit, a radio set to 145.050 MHz, and a GPS.
