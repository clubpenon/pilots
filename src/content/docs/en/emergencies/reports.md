:::caution
The reporting platform and form are a work in progress. This page will be updated shortly.
:::

All [Instructors](/en/lists/authorized-pilots/#instructors), Assistants, [Guides](/en/lists/authorized-pilots/#guides), and [Tandem Pilots](/en/lists/authorized-pilots/#tandem-pilots) have the obligation to report their emergencies to the club at [info@clubpenon.org](mailto:info@clubpenon.org).

Instructors, Assistants, Guides, and Tandem Pilots, as well as their clients, will be required to file an accident report, for which a form will be provided.

Other witnesses are welcome to file a report, but are not required to.
