The El Peñón flight zone is made up of the launch and the official landing zone, El Piano, a big grass field 2.5 km in front of it.

|  | Launch | Landing zone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name | [El Peñón](https://maps.app.goo.gl/ZGx3ansXWHS7XEDcA) | [El Piano](https://maps.app.goo.gl/XcLwxEPTXT3t6SBm8) |
| Altitude | 2,350 m | 1,800 m |

## Launch

The launch was expanded in 2008 for the 2009 Paragliding World Championship and covered with artificial turf in 2025.

- **Wind:** at launch, it blows from the southwest 99% of the time. A Holfuy weather station and webcam are installed at launch: see [Weather](/en/site-info/weather/).
- **Rigging:** don't rig or leave flying equipment on the artificial turf. Use the [rigging areas](/en/rules/facilities/#rigging-areas).
- **Parking:** there's no parking on Club property, and the [loading and unloading area](/en/rules/facilities/#loading-and-unloading-area) is limited to 5 minutes.
- **In the air:** follow the [turning direction](/en/rules/flying/#turning-direction) around launch, and check when [top landing](/en/rules/flying/#top-landing) is allowed.
- **Getting there:** by shared taxi or by car, see [Travel information](/en/site-info/travel/#from-valle-de-bravo-to-launch).

## Landing zone

- **Wind:** it blows from the southwest all day and often shifts to the west at sunset.
- **Kiting:** allowed, but landing pilots always have priority; see the [kiting rules](/en/rules/flying/#kiting).
- **Access:** the access road is signposted from the main road. To get back up to launch, see [Travel information](/en/site-info/travel/#from-the-el-piano-landing-zone).
- **Food and lodging:** Very Muy Épico is a 2-minute walk from the landing zone, and Hangar 5 (which has its own landing zone) a 5-minute walk; see [Pilot-owned businesses](/en/site-info/travel/#pilot-owned-businesses).
