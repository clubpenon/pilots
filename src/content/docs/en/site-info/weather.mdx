## Season and typical conditions

El Peñón is flyable year-round, some 350 days a year. The season runs from November (end of the rainy season) to June (end of the dry season), and the best months are December and January.

- **Dry season:** conditions get stronger, with rough air and strong winds, especially between 12:00 and 16:00. High pressure dominates and good days come further apart.
- **Rainy season:** conditions are mellow, but the cloudbase is low and the flying window is short because of overdevelopment. It rains most afternoons.

### Wind

- At launch, the wind blows from the southwest 99% of the time. Winds aloft are often from the north, but the lower layer, launch included, still blows from the southwest and launch stays usable.
- In the landing zone, the wind blows from the southwest all day and often shifts to the west at sunset.

### Daily cycle

- Thermal cycles at launch typically build from light around 10:00 to strong by 13:00.
- The valley wind picks up significantly in the afternoon.
- The cloudbase can reach 4,500 m; the landing zone is at 1,800 m.
