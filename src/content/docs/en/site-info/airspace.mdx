Know these restrictions before you fly cross-country.

For your flight instrument, download <a href="/airspace.txt" download>airspace.txt</a> (OpenAir format). It contains both the airspace and the prohibited landing zones below.

## Airspace

| Zone | Floor | Ceiling | Area |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Restricted | 4,115 m (13,500 ft) AMSL | Unlimited | East of El Peñón and Valle de Bravo, over Amanalco, Los Saucos and Zinacantepec |
| Restricted | 3,810 m (12,500 ft) AMSL | Unlimited | North of Valle de Bravo, over Villa Victoria and Almoloya de Juárez |
| [Valle de Bravo aerodrome (MX69)](https://maps.app.goo.gl/P58Zmo5TqyGWE4B49) | Surface | 300 m (1,000 ft) AGL | In the El Fresno / Jovan area, behind the gas station and karting track |

## Prohibited landing zones

Landing is strictly prohibited at:

- **[Valle de Bravo aerodrome](https://maps.app.goo.gl/P58Zmo5TqyGWE4B49)**, the small-aircraft airstrip in the El Fresno / Jovan area.
- **[Marina (Armada de México) facilities](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Dh9u6cH71NittsN79)**, on the road up to Mesa Rica in Cuadrilla de Dolores. This includes the heliport, the gas station's parking and maneuvering yard, and the land on the other side of the road. All of it is federal property.
- **[Military base (ex Rancho Las Mesas)](https://maps.app.goo.gl/5t6Lx28c8M3XYpCM9)**.

In the airspace file, the Marina facilities and the military base are coded as "glider prohibited" zones with a 250 m radius, from the ground up to 100 m (330 ft) AGL. Instruments display them differently: this page is the reference.
