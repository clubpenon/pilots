## From Mexico City to Valle de Bravo

### By taxi

A taxi from Mexico City airport to Valle de Bravo costs between 2,500 and 3,500 MXN (≈ 135–190 USD).

### By bus

There is no direct bus from the airport.

<Steps>

1. Take a taxi or public transport to the [Central de Autobuses Observatorio](https://maps.app.goo.gl/ZioN1YPpNvWA7kMk6) bus terminal (also known as Poniente).

2. Take a [Zina bus](https://autobuseszinacantepec.com.mx/) (Autobuses Zinacantepec) to Valle de Bravo. Make sure to take the direct bus: the other service is much slower, as it makes many stops. The trip takes about 2.5 hours, and tickets can be bought online or at the terminal.

</Steps>

## From Valle de Bravo to launch

Launch is about 40 minutes from Valle de Bravo. There is no bus or official transport.

### Shared taxi

Most pilots share a taxi to launch. Everybody meets at the [corner of the church in Santa María Ahuacatlán](https://maps.app.goo.gl/fWjmAPEp6otUq11i6), which is also a good place for a quick breakfast.

- A taxi costs about 350 MXN (≈ 19 USD), shared by 3 or 4 pilots.
- Most pilots leave between 8:30 and 9:30.

### By car

Take the road from Valle de Bravo to Temascaltepec, turn right at the La Albarrada sign, then follow the signs along the dirt road to [launch](https://maps.app.goo.gl/ZGx3ansXWHS7XEDcA).

Any car can make it (taxis go up in old Nissan Tsurus loaded with pilots and gear), but the road is unpaved, so a pickup or an SUV is better. There is no parking on Club property: see the [facility rules](/en/rules/facilities/#parking).

### From the El Piano landing zone

The official landing zone, [El Piano](https://maps.app.goo.gl/XcLwxEPTXT3t6SBm8), is a big grass field 2.5 km in front of launch; its access road is signposted from the main road. To get back up to launch, pilots usually ride with schools and tour operators, who often have spare seats. Hangar 5 also offers transport to launch.

## Pilot-owned businesses

Places to stay, eat and play, all owned by pilots from our local flying community. Choosing them supports the people who share the sky with you.
