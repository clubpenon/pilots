## Top landing on a tandem flight

Sanctions for the pilot:

- **First offense:** 1,500 MXN.
- **Second offense:** 1,500 MXN and a 3-month suspension.
- **Third offense or more:** At the discretion of the Board of Directors, with a minimum 6-month suspension.

## Student accident caused by not respecting flight hours

Sanctions for the instructor:

- **First offense:** 3-month suspension of the right to provide instruction.
- **Second offense:** 6-month suspension of the right to provide instruction.
- **Third offense or more:** At the discretion of the Board of Directors, with a minimum 1-year suspension.

## Exceeding the time limit in the loading area

Sanctions for the pilot:

- **Each offense:** Barred from entering the loading and unloading area on the next visit.
- **5 accumulated offenses:** Access to the loading and unloading area suspended for 12 months, counted from the date of the last offense. When the suspension ends, the count resets.
