This section sets out the rules everyone at the club follows. Some may differ from what you're used to at home, so **please read them before your visit**. They keep the club safe and enjoyable for everyone, and by visiting you agree to follow them.

If anything is unclear, just ask the staff or contact us at [info@clubpenon.org](mailto:info@clubpenon.org).
