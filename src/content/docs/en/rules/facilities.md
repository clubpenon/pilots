## Parking

The Club has an area for loading and unloading equipment, but no parking: you must park outside the Club's property.

Use of the loading and unloading area at launch:

- Maximum stay: **5 minutes**.
- Exceeding it bars the pilot from entering the area on their next visit.
- 5 accumulated violations suspend access for 12 months, counted from the date of the last violation. When the suspension ends, the count resets.

## Rigging areas

**Do not rig or leave flying equipment on the artificial turf.**

- The paraglider rigging area is on the left side of the launch entrance (to the east).
- The hang glider rigging area is on the right side (to the west).

The Club may move these areas at its discretion during competitions or other events.

## Restrooms

The restrooms are in the outside parking area. Urinating in the vegetation or green areas near launch is prohibited.

## Illicit substances and smoking

The following are prohibited on Club premises:

- using illicit substances;
- smoking of any kind, except e-cigarettes and vapes, because of the damage it can do to equipment (paragliders and hang gliders) and because it can burn the artificial turf.

In the dry season, matches and cigarettes can start fires.

## Dogs

Dogs are allowed on a leash, but must stay off the artificial turf.
