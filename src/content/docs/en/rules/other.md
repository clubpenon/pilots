## Agreement with the Club de Vuelo y Vela de Valle de Bravo

Rules in force under the agreement between the clubs:

1. Pilots of the Club de Vuelo y Vela de Valle de Bravo are allowed to fly at El Peñón free of charge, solo only, and must present their membership card.
2. Tandem pilots of that club may fly tandem from El Peñón without being Club Members of Club Peñón if the following conditions are met:
   - The La Torre launch must be closed.
   - They must present a valid tandem pilot card.
   - They must have purchased a 1-day entrance pass.

Claiming that La Torre is closed does not automatically grant access or use of the launch for tandem flights. The closure of La Torre must be verified by a member of the Board of Directors of the Club de Vuelo Libre El Peñón.

In exchange for these privileges, the agreement allows Club Peñón solo pilots to use the Santa María landing zone.

Although the agreement does not provide for it, the Club's current administration has decided to allow pilots of the Club de Vuelo y Vela de Valle de Bravo to fly courtesy tandems (free of charge) at El Peñón. Please note that any abuse will lead to this exception being reconsidered.

:::tip
To fly commercial tandems at El Peñón beyond what the agreement provides for, every pilot must:

1. pay the solo or tandem annual membership fee (see [Fees](/en/rules/fees/));
2. be accepted as a Club Member at an Ordinary General Assembly;
3. be authorized by the Club's Technical Committee.
:::
