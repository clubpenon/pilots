All fees are in Mexican pesos (MXN). All entrance passes work with a QR code.

## Entrance passes

There are two kinds of entrance passes: Season Passes and Annual Passes.

### Season Passes

| Season Pass | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| 1 day | 300 MXN |
| 7 days | 1,500 MXN |
| 15 days | 1,700 MXN |
| 1 month | 2,200 MXN |
| 2 months | 2,800 MXN |
| 3 months | 3,200 MXN |
| 4 months | 3,500 MXN |

Your Season Pass is activated on your first check-in and stays valid for the duration specified, regardless of how many times you check in. After this period, it expires automatically.

Buy it in the [Club shop](https://clubpenon.org/products/season-passes).

### Annual Passes (membership)

| Annual Pass | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| Monoplaza (solo) | 4,000 MXN |
| Tandem | 8,000 MXN |
| Instructor | 8,000 MXN |

Annual Passes follow the calendar year: they are valid from January 1st to December 31st. They are activated the moment you buy them.

Besides letting you fly all year at a lower cost, the Annual Pass gives you the option of becoming a Club Member, with the right to vote in the Assemblies.

Buy it in the [Club shop](https://clubpenon.org/products/membership-passes).

:::note
So that pilots who arrive late in the year don't have to pay a full annual fee to fly only a few months, if you buy your Annual Pass on or after October 1st, it expires on December 31st of the next year.
:::

#### Discount on your first Annual Pass

Your first Monoplaza Annual Pass has a 50% discount, also if you buy it on or after October 1st. Annual Passes follow the calendar year, but many pilots arrive mid-year; the discount spares them paying for a full year when they will use half of it or less.

The discount is only applied in person, at the launch entrance.

#### Occasional commercial tandems

If you only fly commercial tandems occasionally, instead of the Tandem Annual Pass you can hold the Monoplaza Annual Pass and buy a 1-day Season Pass on each day you fly commercial tandems. In both cases you must be a Club Member and an [Authorized Tandem Pilot](/en/lists/authorized-pilots/#tandem-pilots).
